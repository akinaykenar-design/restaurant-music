# Building your music library (free, restaurant-safe)

Target vibe for Watermans: **chilled, relaxed, slightly Mediterranean, organic
electronic — think Four Tet.** Nothing crazy. Lunch + dinner.

## Safest licence for a restaurant
Use **Pixabay** (pixabay.com/music). Its licence is **free for commercial use,
no attribution, and covers public / in-venue playback** — so **no APRA AMCOS or
PPCA fees**. CC0 tracks (e.g. some on Free Music Archive) are equally safe.
Avoid anything only cleared for "YouTube videos / content creation" — that
wording doesn't always cover playing in a venue.

> Rule of thumb: if it's on Pixabay's music library, it's safe for the venue.

## The magic genre: **Organic House**
On Pixabay, the **Organic House** tag *is* your sound — warm pads, hand
percussion, melodic, downtempo, Mediterranean-sundown feel (the free cousin of
Four Tet / Bedouin / Ben Böhmer). Start here:

- **Organic House** — https://pixabay.com/music/search/organic%20house/  ← main
- **Downtempo** — https://pixabay.com/music/search/downtempo/
- **Chill House** — https://pixabay.com/music/search/chill%20house/
- **Ambient Chillout** — https://pixabay.com/music/search/ambient%20chillout/
- **Lounge** — https://pixabay.com/music/search/lounge/
- **Ethnic** (for the Mediterranean spice) — https://pixabay.com/music/search/ethnic/

> Pixabay blocks bulk listing, so rather than a fixed track list that goes

## How to pick the good ones (skip the filler)
- **~90–115 BPM**, relaxed groove.
- **Instrumental** or chopped-vocal only (no cheesy full vocals).
- Warm pads + **real-sounding hand percussion / organic textures**.
- **Avoid** anything tagged *corporate*, *energetic*, *uplifting*, *motivational*.

## A simple plan for lunch + dinner
Grab ~15–20 tracks and split into two playlists:
- **Lunch** — a touch brighter/livelier organic house + lounge.
- **Dinner** — warmer, slower, more downtempo/ambient, a little more Mediterranean.

Then on the **Schedule** tab: Lunch playlist ~11:00, Dinner playlist ~17:00.
The box switches automatically.

## Loading them onto the box
Download the MP3s, then in the app: **Playlists** tab → **drag & drop** them into
the Library → add to the Lunch/Dinner playlists. Done — no cables or file
copying needed.
