## ⚖️ Is it legal to play at the restaurant? (Australia)

Playing recorded music to the public in a café/restaurant **normally** requires a
**OneMusic Australia** licence (it bundles APRA AMCOS — the songwriting/publishing
rights — and PPCA — the sound-recording rights).

You avoid that fee **only** when every track is licensed in a way that already
grants commercial public performance with no royalties owed. The safe categories:

- **Pixabay Content License** — free for commercial use, no attribution required,
  explicitly allows use as background music. ✅ Our default source.
- **CC0 / Public Domain** — no rights reserved. ✅
- **Creative Commons BY / BY-SA** — free commercially but you **must credit** the
  artist. Usable, just noisier to manage. ⚠️ (credit noted per track)

**Watch-outs the agent must check per track:**
- Avoid anything whose creator is an **APRA AMCOS** member — even "royalty-free"
  copies can still fall under APRA. CC0 / Pixabay / public-domain sidestep this.
- Some libraries (Uppbeat free tier, some YouTube Audio Library tracks) require
  **attribution** or restrict certain uses — record the exact terms.
- "Royalty-free to *buy*" ≠ free. We only want $0, publicly-performable tracks.

> This is practical guidance, not legal advice. Keep this list + each track's
> licence page as your paper trail. When in doubt, a OneMusic licence is the
> catch-all.

## ⬇️ Getting tracks into the app

There are two paths, depending on the source:

**A. Pixabay tracks (the batch above)** — no direct file link, so download by hand:
1. Open each link → click **Download** on Pixabay (free)
2. Drag the whole batch into the app's **Library** tab (or drop them into the
   Pi's `music/` folder)
3. **Library → ✨ Analyse audio**, then **✨ Build vibe playlists** / **Auto-fill
   schedule**

**B. CC0 / direct-link tracks (e.g. Internet Archive)** — auto-download with the
included script. Add the direct file URLs to `music-sources.txt`, then, **on the
Pi or your laptop** (open internet — a locked sandbox can't reach these hosts):
```
node scripts/import-music.js
```
The files land straight in `music/`; then Analyse as above. See the top of
`music-sources.txt` for details.
